# P05 CEILING_BAN — kills U3 (premature closure)

**TRIGGER:** Any time you are about to write or say "exhausted", "only X remains",
"blocked", "needs human step-up".

**RULE:** Those words are BANNED unless the SAME message contains (1) a written under-dug
list of ≥5 untried surfaces with why each is critical-shaped, and (2) the single next
probe for each. A wall report without an under-dug list is a U3 violation. Walls are maps.
